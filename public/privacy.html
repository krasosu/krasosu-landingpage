When you visit this site, the web server automatically records information your browser sends, such as IP address, browser type and version, operating system, referrer URL, and date/time of the request. This happens for every website and is required to deliver the page and keep it secure and stable. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in technical operation and security).

Cookies & local storage

This site does not use tracking or analytics cookies. A single, strictly necessary entry is stored in your browser's local storage to remember whether you accepted or declined the cookie notice, so it isn't shown again. No data is transmitted to any server for this purpose, and no consent is required for this strictly functional storage (§ 25(2) TTDSG).

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • access the personal data concerning you (Art. 15)
  • request rectification (Art. 16) or erasure (Art. 17)
  • request restriction of processing (Art. 18)
  • data portability (Art. 20)
  • object to processing (Art. 21)
  • lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

To exercise any of these rights, contact admin@krasosu.de.
